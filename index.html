A bit about me

I chose Natural Sciences because I didn't want to study just one subject. At UCL, I've combined chemistry and biomedical sciences with programming, which has given me a mix of scientific, quantitative and technical experience.

Outside university, I've deliberately explored very different industries. At Legal & General, I used R to analyse the potential insurance implications of obesity drugs. At KPMG, I explored how AI could affect different areas of professional services. I've also gained exposure to investment research and I'm continuing to explore engineering and technology.

Alongside that, I enjoy making things myself. I've worked on a travel-planning web application, built websites and used Python and R for smaller analytical projects.

I don't see science, technology and business as completely separate interests. What interests me most is where they overlap.
